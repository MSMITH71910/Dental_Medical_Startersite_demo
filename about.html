Our Story

Serving Columbus Families Since 1999

Bright Smile Dental & Medical Center was founded in 1999 by Dr. Sarah Mitchell and Dr. James Chen with a shared vision: to bring comprehensive dental and medical care together under one roof, making it easier for Columbus families to stay healthy.

What started as a small two-provider practice has grown into a thriving center with four board-certified providers, a dedicated administrative team, and over 18,000 patients who trust us with their care. Our integrated approach means better communication, coordinated treatment plans, and outstanding outcomes for every patient.

"Our mission is simple: treat every patient like family, deliver results that change lives, and build a healthier Columbus — one smile at a time."

Patient-First Philosophy

Every decision we make starts with what's best for the patient. We listen, explain, and collaborate on care plans that respect your goals and preferences.

Evidence-Based Excellence

We invest in the latest technology and ongoing education so every treatment you receive reflects the best that modern medicine and dentistry have to offer.

Trust & Transparency

We believe you deserve to understand your diagnosis, your treatment options, and your costs — no surprises, no pressure, ever.
