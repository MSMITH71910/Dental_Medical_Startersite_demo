General & Preventive Dentistry

The foundation of a healthy smile starts with consistent preventive care. Our general dentistry services are designed to catch problems early, protect your teeth, and keep your smile healthy for life.

  • Comprehensive oral exams & digital X-rays
  • Professional teeth cleanings (prophylaxis)
  • Dental sealants for cavity prevention
  • Fluoride treatments & oral cancer screenings
  • Fillings, extractions & tooth-colored restorations
  • Gum disease treatment & periodontal maintenance

Urgent Care

When you can't wait for a scheduled appointment, our walk-in urgent care is here for you. No appointment necessary — just walk in during our urgent care hours and our team will take care of you.
